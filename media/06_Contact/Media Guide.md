# CONTACT — Media Guide
**Section:** Sixth section — the contact form and social media links

---

## What it is
The Contact section contains only a contact form, social media link buttons, and text.
**No images are currently used in this section.**

---

## If you want to add an image in the future
1. Place your image file in this folder.
2. Add an `<img>` tag to the `#contact` section in `index.html`.

---

## Recommended specs (if adding an image)

| Setting       | Value                                                   |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Format        | JPEG or PNG                                             |
| Resolution    | **1200 × 800 px** or similar landscape                  |
| File size     | Keep under 300 KB                                       |

---

## Tip
Keeping the Contact section clean and image-free is a deliberate design choice —
it helps focus visitors on taking action (sending a message or clicking a social link).
