# ABOUT — Media Guide
**Section:** Second section — the two-column About layout (your portrait is the left column)

---

## What it is
A vertical portrait photograph displayed in the left column of the About section.
It fills the full height of the column and is lightly processed with desaturation.

---

## How to replace the photo
1. Prepare your portrait photo.
2. **Keep the filename exactly:** `Ananda_Primary.jpeg`  
   *(or update the filename in index.html if you rename it — look for `<img src="...">` inside `.about-photo-wrap`)*
3. Drop the new file into this folder, replacing the old one.

---

## Recommended specs

| Setting       | Value                                                   |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Format        | JPEG (best quality for photos)                          |
| Resolution    | **600 × 900 px** minimum — **1200 × 1800 px** for retina |
| Aspect ratio  | **2:3 — vertical/portrait** (taller than wide)          |
| File size     | Keep under 500 KB (compress in Preview or Squoosh.app)  |

---

## Tips
- Centre your face/subject **vertically** — the bottom of the photo may be cropped on smaller screens.
- A **clean, simple background** works well behind the text.
- The website applies a subtle **greyscale filter** (12%) and slight contrast boost — factor that in when choosing the photo.
- Natural light or a soft studio setup tends to work best.
