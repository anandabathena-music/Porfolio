# STUDIO — Media Guide
**Section:** Fifth section — the full-width banner image with your quote text overlaid

---

## What it is
A full-width landscape photo displayed behind the studio/quote section.
Your quote and subtitle text are overlaid on top of the image.

---

## How to replace the photo
1. Prepare your photo.
2. **Keep the filename exactly:** `Ananda_WorkingStill.jpeg`  
   *(or update the filename in index.html if you rename it — look for `<img id="studio-img" src="...">` inside the `#studio` section)*
3. Drop the new file into this folder, replacing the old one.

---

## Recommended specs

| Setting       | Value                                                         |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| Format        | JPEG (best for photographs)                                   |
| Resolution    | **1920 × 1080 px** minimum — **2560 × 1440 px** for retina   |
| Aspect ratio  | **16:9** (wide landscape)                                     |
| File size     | Keep under 1 MB (compress in Preview or Squoosh.app)          |

---

## Tips
- **Text is overlaid on top** — avoid busy, cluttered areas in the region where your quote sits.
- Darker or slightly blurred areas behind the text improve readability.
- Keep your main subject (you, your studio, your gear) to one side — the other side carries the text.
- A studio or recording environment photo creates the strongest atmosphere for this section.
- Landscape orientation is required — portrait photos will be stretched or cropped.
