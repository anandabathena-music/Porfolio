About

Ananda
Bathena

Alumnus, IDC School of Design, IIT Bombay

I'm a multidisciplinary sound artist with a foundation in filmmaking, VR, and design thinking, creating immersive, story-driven audio experiences. My work spans feature films, advertising, original music, and XR.

I composed and designed sound for the award-winning 6DoF VR experience Into the Manhole, recognized at FICCI BAF, Courant3D 2025, Laval Virtual and Busan International Short Film Festival.
